# Historical Assumptions

This document separates what is **documented** from what is **interpreted** in
the model. Nothing in the "interpretation" column may be quoted as a fact
about the built temple.

## B. Interpreted (modelling decisions, not historical claims)

| # | Decision | Value | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Plan origin and axis | origin = centre of the sanctum, X = east–west | the sanctum is the only fixed, published, symmetric datum |
| B2 | Distance from the sanctum to the west enclosure wall | 45.0 m | the compound is 240.79 m long; the temple block occupies the western part, so the remaining 195.79 m lies to the east of the origin. The published plan does not give the offset |
| B3 | Depth of the courtyard between the Nandi hall and the inner gopuram | ≈ 44 m (Nandi hall east face at +52.7 m, inner gopuram at +100 m) | reconciles the 240.79 m compound length with the published "about 100 m" between the two gateways (the model uses 77 m between them) |
| B4 | Cella plan | 6.4 m square | the linga is 8.7 m and occupies two storeys; a square cella of 6.4 m fits the geometry of a two-storey sanctuary while allowing the 1.8 m ambulatory passage |
| B5 | Sanctuary block height | 12.5 m | consistent with the two-storey cella and the 8.7 m linga |
| B6 | Ambulatory passage width | 1.8 m | a single-file circumambulatory passage, as described |
| B7 | Hall sizes | maha 17.8 × 15.0 m, mukha 11.1 × 11.2 m, Nandi hall 12.0 × 11.0 m | the halls are stated to be square in plan; the maha-mandapa is sized from its six pillars per side (≈ 2.2 m bays) and the Nandi hall from the 5.94 m bull plus circulation |
| B8 | Temple platform | three courses, 1.9 m total | the moulded plinth is documented in section (0.5 m thick) but its height above the courtyard is not published; the model uses a plausible three-course *adhisthana* |
| B9 | Compound wall height / thickness | 3.5 m / 1.2 m | the wall is documented as high and defensive with gun-holes; the model uses a proportion consistent with the photographs |
| B10 | Prakara extent | rectangle from x = −33 m to +140 m, y = ±40 m | gives a 486 m perimeter, close to the published ~450 m |
| B11 | Vimana storey height | 2.87 m | derived so that platform + sanctuary + 13 storeys + crown = 65.83 m |
| B12 | Vimana taper profile | straight with a slight convex bias (exponent 1.35) | photographs show a straight, not curvilinear, profile; the exponent keeps the lower half near vertical as in the images |
| B13 | Number of *kuta* motifs per storey | 4 bays per face, simplified | the real towers carry a dense, irregular series of *kutas* and *salas*; at the model scale a regular rhythm is the only readable abstraction |
| B14 | Pillar proportions | 0.68 m shaft, 0.70 m base, 0.65 m capital | no published pillar dimensions; chosen to read at model scale and to remain printable |
| B15 | Stair geometry | 0.19 m riser, 0.32 m tread, 6 m wide | generic temple stair proportions; the real flights are broader and flanked by balustrades |
| B16 | Subsidiary shrine positions | *Amman* shrine at (72, −14) m, *Murugan* shrine at (72, +14) m | the sources confirm that the shrine of Parvati and of Murugan stand in the courtyard; the exact positions are not published |
| B17 | Vimanā base cover | the 30.18 m aditala is modelled as a solid mass carrying the tower | the real aditala is a constructed mass over the sanctuary and hall roofs; the solid mass reproduces the exterior silhouette and prints cleanly |
| B18 | Cut-away plane | the main axis (Y = 0), southern half removed | the section is a display device, not a reconstruction of any historical cut |

## C. Deliberately not modelled

* The 81 *karana* dance figures of the *aditala* wall (a wall of carved
  figures cannot be resolved at the model scale; the wall itself is present).
* The 670 m² of Chola frescoes (a painted programme, not geometry).
* The brass *Nataraja* and the other later metal images.
* The sixteen *kalasa* finials that surmount the subsidiary shrines.
* The original bronze flagstaff (*dwajasthambam*) visible in the photographs.
* The 1777 gun-holes of the outer wall beyond a general texture.
